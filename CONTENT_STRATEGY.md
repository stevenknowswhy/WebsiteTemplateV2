# Forhem PBC Content Strategy Guide

## Overview

This document outlines the content strategy for the Forhem PBC website, focusing on communicating our mission as a Public Benefit Corporation while driving business objectives.

## Brand Voice and Tone

### Core Brand Attributes

- **Innovative**: Forward-thinking, technology-focused language
- **Community-First**: Emphasis on public benefit and social impact
- **Professional**: Clear, authoritative, and trustworthy
- **Accessible**: Simple explanations of complex technology
- **Transparent**: Honest about capabilities, limitations, and impact

### Voice Guidelines

- Use active voice and direct language
- Avoid jargon where possible; explain technical concepts simply
- Emphasize benefits over features
- Include metrics and data to support claims
- Maintain consistent terminology throughout

### Tone Variations

| Context | Tone | Examples |
|---------|------|----------|
| Homepage | Inspirational, Visionary | "Transforming urban infrastructure", "Building connected communities" |
| Product Pages | Informative, Confident | "Our proprietary technology", "Proven reliability" |
| Investor Relations | Professional, Data-driven | "Q4 revenue grew 45%", "Market penetration of 12 cities" |
| Community Outreach | Welcoming, Educational | "Join us in creating", "Learn how your community can benefit" |
| Technical Documentation | Precise, Clear | "System specifications", "Installation requirements" |

## Content Guidelines

### Messaging Framework

**Problem Statement**:
- Cities lack funding for smart infrastructure
- Digital divide affects underserved communities
- Traditional infrastructure is expensive and slow to deploy

**Solution**:
- Hello Smart Node™: Self-funding infrastructure through revenue sharing
- Solar-powered deployment reduces environmental impact
- Quick installation and immediate community benefits

**Proof Points**:
- 8 successful city deployments
- $2.5M annual revenue run rate
- 45,000+ users connected
- 500+ tons CO2 avoided

**Call to Action**:
- Join pilot program
- Schedule demonstration
- Apply to host nodes
- Invest in growth

### Data and Metrics

**Always Include**:
- Specific numbers and metrics
- Timeframes and dates
- Comparison points (before/after, industry benchmarks)
- Sources and methodology

**Examples**:
- "Connected 12,000 residents in 3 months"
- "45% improvement in emergency response times"
- "$25,000 average annual revenue per property"
- "500 tons CO2 avoided annually"

### Storytelling Elements

**Case Study Structure**:
1. **Challenge**: What problem was solved?
2. **Solution**: How was Forhem's technology implemented?
3. **Results**: Quantifiable outcomes and impact
4. **Testimonial**: Quote from key stakeholder
5. **Future**: Next steps and expansion plans

**Community Impact Stories**:
- Focus on human benefits
- Include personal anecdotes
- Show visual before/after comparisons
- Highlight broader community implications

### Content Approval Process

1. **Content Brief**: Define objectives, key messages, and target audience
2. **Draft Creation**: Write content following brand guidelines
3. **Internal Review**: Technical and brand consistency check
4. **Legal Review**: Ensure compliance and accuracy
5. **Final Approval**: Stakeholder sign-off
6. **Publish**: Schedule and publish content
7. **Promote**: Distribute across channels
8. **Analyze**: Review performance and optimize

### Content Governance

**Style Guide**:
- Consistent terminology and definitions
- Formatting and structure guidelines
- Image and visual content standards
- Tone and voice examples

**Approval Authority**:
- **Technical Content**: CTO or engineering lead
- **Financial Content**: CFO or finance team
- **Marketing Content**: CMO or marketing lead
- **Legal Content**: Legal counsel or compliance officer

## Crisis Communication

### Preparedness

**Potential Scenarios**:
- Service outage or technical failure
- Data breach or security incident
- Negative press or controversy
- Regulatory investigation
- Natural disaster impact

**Response Framework**:
1. **Acknowledge**: Quickly acknowledge the situation
2. **Inform**: Provide accurate, timely information
3. **Action**: Detail steps being taken to resolve
4. **Update**: Maintain regular communication
5. **Resolve**: Confirm resolution and next steps
